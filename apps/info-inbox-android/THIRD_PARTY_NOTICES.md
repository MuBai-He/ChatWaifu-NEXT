# Bundled collector runtime

`assets/collector-runtime.tar` contains the ARM64 phone-local SQLCipher CLI and
its shared-library dependencies. It contains no private `config.sh`, account
identity, source database key, or message archive.

`provenance.json` inside the archive records the exact Termux package URLs,
versions and SHA-256 digests used for the binaries. The archive includes the
upstream notices under `notices/` for SQLCipher, libedit, ncurses and zlib.
OpenSSL 3.6.5 uses Apache-2.0; its upstream license is retained in
`third-party/openssl/LICENSE.txt` and `notices/openssl/LICENSE.txt` in the archive.
Upstream source: https://github.com/openssl/openssl/tree/openssl-3.6.5.

The Gradle wrapper is generated by Gradle and retains its Apache-2.0 notices.
Robolectric/JUnit and other build/test dependencies are resolved by Gradle and
are not part of the phone's collector runtime.
